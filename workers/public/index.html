Questions, answered

The short version of everything people ask us.

What does "proof" actually mean here?

Every photo the inspector takes is digitally signed at capture, stamped with GPS coordinates and time. The full set is hashed into a single digest. If anyone alters a photo, a timestamp, or a word of the report afterward, the signatures no longer match — and anyone can check that independently. It's the same math that secures online banking, applied to your property decision.

I'm handling a claim — how fast can I get the report?

Reports are delivered within 24–48 hours of the inspection. The report leads with a clear condition summary — built for a quick decision, not a long read. The full signed evidence bundle is attached for your file.

Can I use this in a deposit dispute?

Yes — that's one of the most common uses. The move-in/move-out documentation is timestamped, GPS-sealed, and cryptographically signed, which makes it very hard to dispute. Many landlords tell us disputes end as soon as they share the proof link.

Who performs the inspections?

Licensed local inspectors in our contractor network. Each inspector holds their own signing key, so every piece of evidence is attributable to the person who captured it.

What areas do you serve?

We're starting in Erie, PA and the surrounding region, expanding outward. If you're outside the current area, place an order anyway — we'll tell you honestly if we can't reach you yet.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Reply to your confirmation email any time before the inspector arrives. Full refund if we haven't dispatched; rescheduling is always free.
