Questions, answered

The short version of everything people ask us.

What does "cryptographically verified" actually mean?

Every photo the inspector takes is digitally signed at capture with their private key, and stamped with GPS coordinates and time. The full set is hashed into a single digest. If anyone alters a photo, a timestamp, or a word of the report afterward, the signatures no longer match — and anyone can check that independently. It's the same math that secures online banking, applied to your property.

Who performs the inspections?

Licensed local inspectors in our contractor network. Each inspector holds their own signing key, so every piece of evidence is attributable to the person who captured it.

How do I verify the proof myself?

Your report page includes the proof bundle download and the published digest. We provide a verification guide — you don't need special software, and you don't have to take our word for anything.

What areas do you serve?

We're starting in Erie, PA and the surrounding region, expanding outward. If you're outside the current area, place an order anyway — we'll tell you honestly if we can't reach you yet.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Reply to your confirmation email any time before the inspector arrives. Full refund if we haven't dispatched; rescheduling is always free.
